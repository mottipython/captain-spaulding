# Captain Spaulding: Gemini prompts

A toy-theatre paper-doll show: jointed cardboard figures held together with brass split pins, on a cardboard safari stage. The code moves the parts around their pins, so each figure comes as a sheet of separate parts.

Copy each block whole into Gemini and save the result in `~/Pictures/captain-spaulding/` under the name in the heading.
